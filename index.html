HOW TO PLAY

  • 🎯 Everyone sees the same question. First correct answer wins it: +100 plus a speed bonus (up to +100).
  • 💥 Wrong answer: −50 and you're locked out of that question — and your pick is stamped on everyone's screen, so you've just eliminated an answer for your rivals.
  • ⏱️ Timer per question, rounds, and difficulty are set by the host. 10 questions per round. Most points at the end wins!
  • 👑 ROYALE mode is endless: everyone starts with 1000 points and antes into a pot each question (25, 50 or 100 — the host sets the pace) — the question winner takes the pot (it rolls over if nobody's right). Hit 0 and you're out, spectating. Last one standing wins. The ramp dial goes 1 (one fixed difficulty) to 5 (total chaos).
  • 👻 Ghost shot (Royale): if every survivor gets a question wrong, the fallen get one chance to answer it — nail it and you RISE AGAIN with 100 points.
  • ⚔️ Duel (Royale): challenge a foe and stake 0-200. You alternate solo untimed questions; the first miss loses and pays the stake. Can't cover it? You bust out.
